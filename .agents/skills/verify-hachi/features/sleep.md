# Sleep comparison

## Sub-features

Steady evening, later night, unsupported schedule; assumptions; paired curves and tables.

## How to get to it (user POV)

Click Sleep comparison; no report is needed.

## Driving it with CUA

Select each option in `Named fixture preset`. Inspect both supported schedules, bedtime/wake date/timezone and fixed sleep/caffeine/light assumptions. Require both curves to share a 0 to 100 illustration scale; compare each plot with its expanded exact-value table. Unsupported must show unavailable without a curve. At phone width, focus the named chart region and press ArrowRight: its scroll position should change while the document stays within the viewport.

## Gotchas

Static examples do not calculate personalized alertness or establish validated accuracy. Preserve the unsupported state rather than substituting another curve.
