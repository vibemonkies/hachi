# Prospective preparation protocol 0.1

Frozen preparation choices, 2026-10-11. This is a software protocol, not an approved study, a #20 model freeze, or evidence of independent validation. No retrospective friend case was accessed or used to select these endpoints. A real study requires a separately authorized, reviewed final protocol before recruitment, any outcome scoring, or opening untouched periods.

## Implemented measurement

Record one **next completed local day** forecast before that day's local midnight, using the protocol's IANA timezone and canonical day bounds (including 23/25-hour DST days). Explicitly freeze each metric and its canonical aggregation in `Protocol.targets`. First planned primary target: vendor resting heart rate, mean, in bpm; secondary: sleep duration (`sleep_asleep_unspecified`), sum, in seconds. These source-specific labels are not interchangeable with overnight resting HR or staged sleep. Generated proof uses only the primary target and makes no accuracy claim.

Save the canonical `ReportRequest` and `ReportResult` verbatim, not a summary reconstructed afterward. Require request/result subject, task and cutoff equality; every requested metric must appear as a forecast or explicit unavailable metric. Successful forecasts must match resolution, timezone, exact target bounds, selected artifact, code and input snapshot. Record source snapshot/version/hash and a checksum of the exact materialized input document. That checksum does not verify causal feature construction or inference: the upstream producer must supply and verify those independently. No post-cutoff feature material or unverified available-at assumptions may be admitted to a future real study.

The local protocol registration precedes the request cutoff. Generated result time is between cutoff and saving time. Save within **60 seconds** of cutoff and strictly before the target window starts; the configurable maximum may not exceed 300 seconds and is frozen at registration. A missed slot is an exclusion, never a prediction backfill. No retry with refreshed pins may replace a saved prediction. An existing report, exact request, or subject/task/window cannot be logged again.

Record follow-up only after the saved prediction. Complete follow-up requires observation through the full target day, an actual recording time no earlier than observed-through time, the exact canonical aggregate list and its checksum, a versioned outcome snapshot, matching subject/timezone/metric/aggregation/window and all forecast targets. Preserve canonical null value/reason/coverage. Missing and right-censored follow-up require a named reason and contain no invented aggregate. A missing day is finalized after its end; withdrawal/lost follow-up can be censored earlier. Finalized follow-up is single-use; amendments are a future protocol decision, not an edit or a hidden replacement here. Clinical successful results and clinical outcome scoring are refused in this version; canonical unavailable/error results may be retained as unscored availability evidence.

## Analysis requirements before real scoring

Primary: resting-HR MAE in bpm, averaged within participant and then across participants, with a participant-bootstrap 95% interval and paired difference from an identical-window last-valid-value and seasonal-naive comparator. Retain zero/negative improvements. Secondary sleep-duration MAE/RMSE and per-metric interval coverage/width, conditional on coverage and with counts. Do not compute interval coverage for point-only outputs. Calibration parameters and thresholds come from earlier development/validation only, pinned before the future period; no recalibration on the evaluation period.

Prespecify a fixed 28-day evaluation block and a fixed calendar end, with no stopping at a favorable result. Report participants, scheduled days, saved predictions, missed logging slots, unavailable/error results, observed outcomes, missing/censored days, null aggregates, and every exclusion. Missing labels are unknown, never healthy or negative. Report coverage, abstention and errors by source/device, coverage stratum and protocol/model version. Compare each block's feature coverage, missing channels, warning counts and source/device mix against the frozen development reference; report drift descriptively and do not tune or retrain automatically on drift.

Future clinical extension requires its own preregistration before enabling success: onset within separately scored 24/48/72-hour windows with full uncertainty interval inside the prespecified half-open window, exclusion of ongoing episodes, and an explicit symptom-free observed denominator. A positive test is a separate target. Recovery needs the source's prespecified sustained symptom-resolution definition and right-censoring; improvement is not resolution. Freeze the earliest eligible scoring instant relative to actual saved-receipt time, so no onset already occurring when the prediction is saved can be counted as prospective. Record outcome event and collection times separately, confounders and uncertain labels. None of this is clinical support in the implemented daily-forecast journal.

Before clinical scoring, prespecify Brier score, calibration intercept/slope and a smooth reliability curve, AUPRC with prevalence, AUROC, sensitivity/PPV at the fixed validation-chosen threshold, and lead time. Alert burden must use merged contiguous alerted-day episodes per 30 **observed healthy** days, alerted-day fraction and false-alert counts; absent surveys stay out of the healthy denominator. Report false alerts, false negatives, successful negatives, device drift and label missingness, including poor results. No clinical claim or treatment action follows from this research prototype.

## Participants, events and precision

No actual sample size is approved or attained. Before recruitment freeze a reproducible precision calculation using the actual selected model's development prediction distribution, expected prevalence, follow-up/missingness, clustering and the target population. Take the largest required size for calibration, discrimination, primary error and operational alert-burden precision. Use simulation or a suitable cluster-aware method for repeated days; 28 days per person are not 28 independent participants. Freeze calculation inputs, code, seed, sample/event requirements and end rule; report achieved versus planned independent participants, onset events, healthy days, censored episodes and each subgroup count.

Planned precision targets to take to review: primary MAE 95% interval half-width at most 1 bpm; sensitivity and specificity half-width at most 0.05; calibration slope interval width at most 0.20 and intercept interval width at most 0.20. These are study-sizing goals, not accuracy promotion thresholds. A conservative independent-binomial normal approximation for half-width 0.05 at probability 0.5 gives `ceil(1.96² × 0.25 / 0.05²) = 385` events for sensitivity and 385 non-events for specificity. This illustrative calculation neither sizes calibration nor accounts for clustering/missingness; it cannot authorize recruitment or justify a universal event-count rule. Sparse events, absent healthy follow-up or inadequate precision mean **unestimable/inconclusive**, not validation. [Riley et al., BMJ 2024](https://www.bmj.com/content/384/bmj-2023-074821) recommends model- and setting-specific precision calculations rather than universal counts. [The companion external-validation methods paper](https://www.bmj.com/content/384/bmj-2023-074820) addresses calibration, discrimination and utility.

## Versioning and independent evidence

`Protocol` freezes model version, artifact digest, producing Git SHA, environment, model config, preprocessing, threshold, calibration, selection-receipt and analysis-plan hashes. Any changed byte requires a new protocol/model version and a new journal registered before its future untouched period. Existing outcomes cannot select the new version's thresholds/endpoints; development and evaluation periods must be explicitly separated, with old version results retained. A #20 `FreezeReceipt` or receipt digest records decisions, **never authorization**: final scientific/test-opening acceptance stays with the root coordinator. No real #20 receipt is fabricated by generated fixtures.

Local receipt timestamps depend on the machine clock. Exclusive 0600 files in a 0700 directory, fsync, a per-directory writer lock, canonical serialization and predecessor hashes provide append-only application behavior and detect changes **against an externally retained expected head**. The owner can change the clock, rewrite an entire chain, omit a prefix in a new journal, or replace both chain and head pin. A retained head detects deletion/rewrite of that pinned history; it does not reveal an unretained future suffix. No signature, trusted timestamp authority, independent custodian, consent verification or independent blinding is implemented. Independent custody/attestation and a separate evaluator are prerequisites to an independently validated claim, not consequences of this local log.

## Recruitment, consent and institutional prerequisites

Before any research expansion, a responsible investigator must obtain the applicable institutional determination, required training, approved recruitment/eligibility materials and data/security/retention/withdrawal procedures; establish study purpose, participant burden, outcomes, contact/safety responsibilities and a consent process appropriate to that determination. Use voluntary, equitable eligibility and separate permission for processing versus presentation; recruit no dependent/vulnerable population without specific review. Do not infer exemption or consent from friend status, app use, a signed hash, public data access or hackathon participation. Keep identities separate, minimize collection, provide withdrawal and deletion handling and decide how to reconcile privacy obligations with scientific retention before enabling a real log.
