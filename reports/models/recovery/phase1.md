# Superseded Stanford recovery evidence

The af7e26b packet and its reported comparison are invalidated by the independent review's B1/B2 findings. B1 used the last answered survey as known-unresolved time, overstating survival when a symptom-free tail or a preceding survey gap could contain the resolution. B2 published a whole-roster eligibility audit that exposed sealed-test outcome aggregates by subtraction.

The old exploratory audit did load whole-roster surveys/recovery labels. That historical exposure cannot be undone by removing its counts. Corrected runs retain the frozen partitions, read outcome/predictor tables only for development participants, and disclose the exposure without republishing test aggregates. No final blind-test claim is supported by this historical Stanford audit.

Old comparison numbers and whole-roster eligibility counts are withdrawn. The original private packet remains unchanged for provenance and must not be consumed as accepted evidence. A corrected clean-source run with the original Stanford model choices and a separate frozen CMU protocol will replace this notice. Clinical serving remains gated.
